# Shelly Codes

Learn real Python with Shelly the turtle. An iPad app for kids aged 8 to 12.

- [Privacy policy](privacy.md): Shelly Codes collects no data at all.
- **Support:** questions, bugs or ideas? Open an issue at
  <https://github.com/polyglot-learn/shelly-codes/issues>.

## For parents and teachers

- 27 short levels in 6 chapters: moving and turning, printing and variables, for
  loops, colors and pens, lists/if/while, and functions.
- Children type real Python. Shelly understands variables, numbers and text, lists,
  `for` and `while` loops, `if`/`elif`/`else`, functions with `def`, and the commands
  from Python's `turtle` module, written exactly as in real Python.
- Mistakes are explained in plain words, on the line where they happen.
- No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no tracking. Works offline.
- In the Grown-ups area (behind a parental gate) you can unlock every level for a
  classroom, or reset progress.
