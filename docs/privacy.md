# Shelly Codes Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

Shelly Codes is an app that teaches children to write Python code. It is made for
children aged 8 to 12, and it is built to collect nothing.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Shelly Codes does not collect, store, send or share any personal
information or usage data. It has:

- no accounts or sign-in
- no advertising
- no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools
- no in-app purchases
- no links to websites or other apps
- no internet connection: the app works fully offline

## What stays on the device

The app saves a child's progress (solved levels, stars and their code) and their
drawings in Free Draw, plus a few settings such as which shell color Shelly
wears. This information is stored only on the iPad, inside the app. It is never
sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. A parent or teacher can
also erase it from the app's Grown-ups area.

If the iPad's own iCloud Backup is turned on, Apple may include the app's data in
that backup, under Apple's privacy policy. We never have access to it.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing
for a parent to review or delete on our side. The Grown-ups area is protected by
a parental gate.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a
new date.
